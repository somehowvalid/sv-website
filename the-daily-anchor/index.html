what it is

one pattern, given time.

A Daily Anchor collection stays with one emotional pattern across thirty days. Not to solve it quickly. To let it become visible from more than one angle.

who it is for

the person still carrying it.

For the pattern that keeps returning after the conversation ends. For the sentence you understand once, then need again. For the part of you that does not need a plan yet. It needs language close enough to return to.

why it exists

because some language needs to stay near.

A single sentence can open the door. A Daily Anchor collection lets the language remain: in a Compass, on your lock screen, and in a deck of words your eye can find again.

why this
format

The Daily Anchor™ is not a challenge, a course, or a practice plan. It is a place for language that may need to be returned to more than once.

The Compass names the pattern. The Wallpapers keep one line close. The Anchor Deck gives the language somewhere physical to live if you choose to print it.
